Five things to try

  1. Open Grants and pick any grant. Its organization, contacts, payments and requirements are linked on one page.
  2. As Staff, add a grant and leave the reference number empty. Within two minutes it gets the next number by itself.
  3. As Staff, try to add an organization named Summit Literacy Alliance, or one with the EIN 00-0001106. The system refuses the copy.
  4. As Manager, edit a grant under Grants awaiting a decision on Home: set Approved, add the amount and a note, then check the History tab.
  5. Open Fiscal Periods for budget versus awarded. On Home, Needs attention shows overdue requirements in colour.

Get the free starter

  • The nine areas, the three roles and sample data, ready to install
  • About 30 minutes, no code, with a how-to video
  • The software is free: no licence, no per-user fee. The only cost is a server: about $13 a month is comfortable with about 8,000 records (for example 1,000 grants with their payments, requirements, organizations and contacts) and usable to about 20,000, and its 25 GB of disk holds about 20,000 one-megabyte documents. Like a laptop, it has a disk for storage and a processor for speed: records barely use the disk, and as they grow it is processing power that slows pages, not disk space or, we found, memory. When you outgrow it, move to a server with two or more processors: in our test, two processors (about $40 a month) kept pages quick for five people at 5,000 grants, while more memory alone did not help. Your data moves with you. Elestio can change its prices; check with Elestio directly
